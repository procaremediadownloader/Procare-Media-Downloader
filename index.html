Procare Media Downloader

Save the photos and videos already shared with your Procare family account straight to your own computer.

What it does

Sign in to Procare the way you normally do, pick a child and a date range, and start the download. Files are saved to your own Downloads folder, sorted by child and month. Anything saved on an earlier run is skipped rather than downloaded twice, so a long archive can be paused and picked up later.

What it does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No cloud storage and no developer server. Your files never pass through anyone else.
  • Never asks for your password. It uses the session you already created by signing in to Procare yourself.
  • No access to any website other than Procare.
  • No remote code. Everything it runs ships inside the extension package.

Privacy

The full privacy policy explains exactly what the extension reads, what it stores on your own machine, and why none of it reaches a developer.

Please be thoughtful with what you download

Photos shared by a childcare centre often include children other than your own. Store them carefully, and do not repost images of other people's children without their families' permission.

Your centre may have its own guidance on sharing media as well.
