How it works

  1. The extension runs only on the Procare family website and only after you open it.
  2. It uses your existing Procare sign-in inside that tab. Your password and session token are never sent to an outside developer or stored by the extension.
  3. It asks Procare for authorized activity records one month at a time, newest first.
  4. Photo and video URLs supplied by Procare are downloaded over HTTPS, one completed file at a time.
  5. Chrome stores only media identifiers and completion times locally so interrupted downloads can resume without starting over.
  6. There is no developer server, cloud upload, analytics, advertising, or remote executable code.
